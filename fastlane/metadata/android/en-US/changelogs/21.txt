Pictures and drawings in a note. « ✎ » at the bottom of the list draws a new note; « ✎ draw » under a note draws at the cursor; ⋯ › « insert a picture » adds one from the gallery, and a picture shared from another app becomes a note. A pen draws with its pressure, its eraser end erases, and the hand resting on the screen leaves no marks. Tap a picture to draw on it again or remove it. Pictures sync with the notes.
